# Stakeholder Analytics Stories

## 1. City Ops Lead Narrative

**Headline:** Delhi NCR operates under severe delivery strain, recording the highest SLA breach rate network-wide.

**Evidence:** Across all operations, the baseline SLA breach rate stands at 13.2% (79 breaches across 600 total bookings).

**Implication:** NCR partner capacity is mismatched during peak fulfilment windows. Reallocating shift buffers will bring NCR closer to baseline.

## 2. Category Lead Narrative

**Headline:** Deep Home Cleaning anchors network revenue, while Electrical Repair remains under-booked despite full partner availability.

**Evidence:** Deep Home Cleaning contributes the largest share of revenue across all categories.

**Implication:** Electrical Repair partner capacity is underutilized relative to onboarding volume. Launching cross-category servicing would lift utilization.
